FAQ

Questions We Get All The Time

If yours isn't here, just ask us on a free call.

How long does setup typically take?

+

Most projects are delivered within 2 4 weeks depending on complexity. Simple chatbots can go live in under a week. We'll give you a clear timeline in the discovery call.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

+

None at all. We handle everything from setup to deployment to training. You just tell us what you need, we build it and explain how to use it in plain English.

Can you integrate with our existing CRM?

+

Yes. We integrate with most popular CRMs including GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, and custom databases. We'll confirm compatibility in the discovery call.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

+

Yes all projects include at least 30 days of support. Growth and Enterprise clients get extended support, regular performance reviews, and continuous optimisation.

Can the AI handle calls in Hindi or regional languages?

+

Absolutely. Our voice agents support Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Kannada, and more we can deploy multilingual agents tailored to your customer base.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

+

All systems are built with guardrails and fallback logic. Complex or sensitive cases are escalated to a human automatically. We also monitor and improve performance continuously.
